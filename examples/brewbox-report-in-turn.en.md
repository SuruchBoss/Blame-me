Panel: an early-stage investor, the HR manager of a 30-person office (who decides to pay), and an accountant. The panel was played in turn by one model, because no separate helpers were available. This is less independent than separate readers. They saw `proposal.th.md` and nothing else. There was no brief or criteria.

**Verdicts**
- Investor: Fail. Each sale loses money by the work's own numbers. The 60% profit claim and the "no major risks" claim don't hold, so the plan can't support putting 1.5 million baht in.
- HR manager: Fail. The work doesn't show why the company should pay 4,900 baht a month for something staff now buy themselves. It also doesn't say who deals with the equipment or what happens if people stop using it.
- Accountant: Fail. The cost table doesn't match the usage figures in the same document, and the stated profit can't be rebuilt from it.

**Weak spots**

1. [Must fix first · 3/3] Each office loses money every month
   Where in the work: "พนักงานออฟฟิศดื่มกาแฟเฉลี่ยคนละ 2 แก้วต่อวัน การดริปหนึ่งแก้วใช้เมล็ดกาแฟ 15 กรัม" (Customer usage) + "ราคาส่ง 600 บาทต่อกิโลกรัม" (Costs) + "กำไรขั้นต้นประมาณ 60%" (Costs) + "4,900 บาทต่อเดือน" (Summary)
   When it goes wrong: For a 20-person office, 20 people × 22 days × 2 cups × 15 g = 13.2 kg of beans a month. At 600 baht per kg that is 7,920 baht. Add shipping (320) and equipment wear (150) and the cost is 8,390 baht against a price of 4,900. That is a loss of about 3,490 baht per office per month, or about 71% of the price, not a 60% profit. Even at half the surveyed usage (1 cup a day), cost is about 4,430 baht, so profit is under 10%. The first paying office already costs more than it brings in. The market section's 58.8 million baht would then mean bigger losses, not bigger profit.
   Quick way to check: Redo the sum above with your own numbers. Then ask your bean supplier for a written price, and weigh the coffee used by 3 real offices over one week.

2. [Should fix · 2/3, HR manager and investor] The "saving" belongs to the staff, not to the one who pays
   Where in the work: "ช่วยออฟฟิศประหยัดค่ากาแฟของพนักงานได้กว่า 70%" (Summary) + "พนักงานออฟฟิศในกรุงเทพซื้อกาแฟเฉลี่ยวันละ 60 บาท" (Problem)
   When it goes wrong: The 26,400 baht is what employees pay out of their own pockets. The company pays nothing today. The HR manager is asked to add a new 58,800 baht a year to the budget. The work does not say what the company gets for it, such as better morale or fewer people leaving the desk. HR will ask "why is this my cost?" and the page has no answer. The 70% also assumes every employee stops buying coffee outside. The work does not say that will happen.
   Quick way to check: Ask 5 HR managers or office managers: "Would you put this in the budget? What would you need to see?" Ask 10 employees how many of their daily coffees they would really swap.

3. [Should fix · 3/3] The funding request doesn't match the plan
   Where in the work: "อุปกรณ์ 300 ชุด" + "การตลาด 6 เดือน (รายละเอียดอยู่ในภาคผนวก ข)" (Funding) + "ชุดดริปพร้อมกาไฟฟ้า 2 ชุดต่อออฟฟิศ" (Solution) + "ทดลองใช้ฟรีเดือนแรก" (Finding customers) + "1,000 ออฟฟิศ" (Market)
   When it goes wrong: 300 sets at 2 per office covers 150 offices. The market section counts 1,000 offices, which needs 2,000 sets. The free first month costs about 8,240 baht per office in beans and delivery, using the work's own usage figures. For 150 offices that is about 1.2 million baht, nearly the whole 1.5 million, before any equipment or marketing. The work does not say what a set costs, and Appendix B is not in the file. An investor will ask where the money goes and the page can't show it.
   Quick way to check: Write one table: number of offices × (cost of a set + free month) + marketing, and compare it with 1.5 million. Get one real price for the kettle and dripper.

4. [Should fix · 2/3, accountant and investor] One flat price, no limit on office size
   Where in the work: "ราคาเดียว 4,900 บาทต่อเดือน" + "ออฟฟิศที่มีพนักงาน 20 คนขึ้นไป" (Summary)
   When it goes wrong: "20 or more" has no upper limit. At the work's own usage, a 100-person office uses about 66 kg of beans a month, which is about 39,600 baht in beans against 4,900 in income. The bigger the customer, the bigger the loss. The work does not say whether there is a cap or an extra charge.
   Quick way to check: Work out the monthly cost for offices of 20, 50 and 100 people. See where the flat price stops making sense.

5. [Should fix · 2/3, investor and HR manager] Nothing shows that customers exist or can be reached
   Where in the work: "จากการสำรวจ" (Customer usage) + "บอกต่อจากลูกค้าเดิม" (Finding customers) + "ทีมมีความพร้อมเต็มที่ในการขายให้องค์กร" (Team) + "มกราคม 2027: หาออฟฟิศนำร่อง 5 แห่ง" (Timeline)
   When it goes wrong: The survey has no sample size or source. The work has no customers yet (the first pilots are planned for January 2027), so there are no "existing customers" to pass the word on. The team is a barista and a graphic designer, and nothing in the work shows anyone has sold to a company before. A company sale usually has to go through HR and finance, and the work doesn't say who the buyer is or how long approval takes.
   Quick way to check: Contact 20 offices this week and count how many agree to a 15-minute call. Note who in each office says yes or no.

6. [Should fix · 2/3, investor and HR manager] "No major risks" is not credible
   Where in the work: "ไม่มีความเสี่ยงสำคัญ เพราะกาแฟเป็นสิ่งที่คนดื่มทุกวัน" (Risks) + "ให้ยืม ไม่ต้องซื้อ" (Solution)
   When it goes wrong: People drinking coffee daily doesn't mean they will drink this coffee, brewed by themselves, at the office. The work does not say who pays if a lent kettle breaks or goes missing, what happens to the equipment when a customer cancels, or whether there is a contract length. It does not say what happens if bean prices rise. Two sets for 20 people who drink about 40 cups a day may also mean a queue. The work does not say how long a cup takes to brew.
   Quick way to check: Brew with 2 sets in a real 20-person office for one day and time the queue. Write down 5 things that could go wrong and what each would cost.

7. [Should fix · 1/3, accountant] The cost table can't be rebuilt, and looks incomplete
   Where in the work: "ค่าเสื่อมอุปกรณ์ | 150 บาท" + "กำไรขั้นต้นประมาณ 60%" (Costs)
   When it goes wrong: The 150 baht equipment wear charge has no equipment price or lifespan behind it, so it can't be checked. The work lists no packing, roasting, payment fees, staff time or tax (VAT). The work does not say whether the 600 baht per kg is for roasted or raw beans. The accountant can't reproduce 60% from any figure on the page.
   Quick way to check: List the cost of one kettle and dripper set and how many months it will last. Ask a supplier whether 600 baht per kg is roasted.

8. [Good to fix · 2/3, investor and accountant] Market figure is large but loosely built
   Where in the work: "กรุงเทพมีออฟฟิศมากกว่า 50,000 แห่ง ถ้าได้ลูกค้าเพียง 2%" (Market)
   When it goes wrong: The sum is correct (1,000 × 4,900 × 12 = 58.8 million). But the work does not say how many of the 50,000 offices have 20 or more staff, which is the only group the product is for. The 2% has no reason behind it. And 58.8 million is income, not profit, which is negative under spot 1.
   Quick way to check: Find the number of Bangkok offices with 20 or more staff in a public business listing. Redo the 2% on that smaller number.

9. [Good to fix · 3/3] The dates and delivery rhythm disagree
   Where in the work: "เปิดตัวเดือนมีนาคม 2027" (Summary) vs "พฤษภาคม 2027: เปิดขายทั่วไป" (Timeline); "ทุกเดือน" (Summary) vs "ส่งถึงออฟฟิศทุกสัปดาห์" (Solution)
   When it goes wrong: A reader sees two launch dates, March and May 2027, and two delivery rhythms, monthly and weekly. Four trips at 80 baht in the cost table matches weekly. Nobody knows which is true.
   Quick way to check: Pick one launch date and one delivery rhythm, then search the document for every mention.

**Already holds up**
- Problem arithmetic: 20 × 60 × 22 = 26,400 baht is correct, and saving against 4,900 baht is about 81%, so "over 70%" is true on those numbers.
- Market arithmetic: 2% of 50,000 is 1,000, and 1,000 × 4,900 × 12 = 58,800,000. The sum is right even though the inputs are weak.
- Shipping line: 4 × 80 = 320 baht is correct.
